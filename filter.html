How to use

You need both of your hands inframe first, make sure that the skeleton of the hands are generating properly.
- i reccommend having your palms facing the camera so the program can read the skeleton better, then touch the fingertips of your thumb and middlefinger together (on each of both hands). That will render the usual filter range.
- Doing the same thing but with your index finger instead of middle will create a pixelated range.
- Filters can stack.
- If you make the usual filter range overlap on each other like an hourglass, the right triangle will be a black-and-green filter, while the left triangle can change normally.


- To change the filter, move your right (or left, depends) index finger how the fingertip will be lower than the knuckle of that finger (like a flick of your finger, pointing down). Make sure to move your finger back into its usual pointing up position, because THEN the filter will change.
